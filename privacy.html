Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Stephen Akintayo Foundation handles information connected with the Billionaire Habits Retreat — Japan Edition website, registration, and payment process.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Payment safety: Registration information may be used to identify participants and help verify and protect payment transactions. Card payments are processed by the payment provider at checkout; this website does not ask for or store your full card number or security code.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Contact and identity details you provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Registration details, selected package, booking reference, and event-related messages.
  • Payment status and transaction references used to confirm and reconcile a booking. Card details are handled by the payment provider.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when loading this site, such as device, browser, and request information.

2. How we use information

We may use information to identify participants, process and administer registrations, confirm package selection, communicate important event updates, answer enquiries, verify and reconcile payments, prevent or investigate suspected fraud or unauthorized transactions, and meet legal obligations.

3. Payment processing

When you select a package, checkout is provided by the payment provider linked from this website (currently Stripe). That provider processes payment information under its own privacy policy and terms. We receive information needed to confirm and support the booking, such as payment status and transaction references. We do not request or store your full payment card number or card security code through this website.

4. When information may be shared

We may share relevant information with staff and service providers who need it to manage registrations, communicate with participants, process payments, protect transactions, or provide the retreat. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect participants, the organizers, and their rights.

We do not state that payment providers or other independent service providers are governed by this policy; please review their own privacy information.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to administer the event, handle payment and registration records, address disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods may vary by record type and legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to limit access to personal information. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Cookies and external services

This website loads some resources, such as fonts, from third-party services. Opening the payment checkout takes you to the payment provider, which may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. Your browser may provide basic technical information to these services when a resource or checkout page is requested.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your registration, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and some records may need to be retained where required by law.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The current version and its revision date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact products@stephenakintayo.com, call +1 281 505 0527, or call +234 816 790 1719.
