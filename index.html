When is the retreat?
Billionaire Habits Retreat — Japan Edition runs from 24th – 30th November 2026. Register and get updates at www.stephenakintayo.com/japanretreat.
What’s the difference between the four packages?
All four include the 7‑day program, tours, and masterminds. Program Only ($3,000) is sessions and tours — you book your own hotel. Program + Concierge ($4,000) adds visa support and concierge help; hotel still not included. Shared Room ($13,000) adds twin/shared luxury accommodation, breakfast, selected dinners, and group transport. Private Room ($16,000) upgrades to a private room, hands‑on visa support, and concierge help. Embassy and government fees are always paid by you.
Do I need a visa to enter Japan?
Most international guests need a visa. Program Only and Shared Room include guidance resources; Program + Concierge and Private Room add hands‑on visa support. Government and embassy fees are paid by you where applicable.
How do I book?
Choose one of the four packages and complete checkout via its Stripe link in the pricing section. Seats are confirmed in order of successful booking (25 max).
What is not included in the price?
International flights to/from Japan, visa processing and embassy fees, personal expenses, and travel insurance (mandatory). Program Only and Program + Concierge also do not include hotel. Shared Room and Private Room include accommodation, breakfast, selected dinners, bullet trains, group transport, tours, and sessions.
Is this retreat suitable for couples?
Yes. However, the retreat is business‑focused. Couples may attend if both are aligned with the retreat purpose. Private Room is recommended if you prefer your own space.
What airport should I fly into?
Final travel guidance will be provided after booking, but you’ll typically arrive in Tokyo (HND/NRT) and depart from Osaka (KIX). VIP airport pickup and group transfers in Japan are included.
Can I extend my stay in Japan?
Absolutely. Many participants choose to add extra days. Our concierge can recommend add‑ons — especially on the Private Room package.
