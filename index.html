Why Japan. Why Now.

Japan is one of the most advanced economies on Earth — not only because of technology, but because of a deep cultural commitment to excellence, systems, and long‑term wealth. It’s not a conference. It’s not tourism. It’s a high‑level retreat designed to reset your thinking, expand your network, and open real business pathways between Africa, the UK, and Asia.

When is the retreat?
Billionaire Habits Retreat — Japan Edition runs from 25th April – 1st May 2027. Register and get updates at www.stephenakintayo.com/japanretreat.
What’s the difference between the two packages?
Both include the 5‑day program, tours, and masterminds. Program Only ($3,000) is sessions and tours — you book your own hotel. Program + Concierge ($4,000) adds visa support and concierge help; hotel still not included. Embassy and government fees are always paid by you.
Do I need a visa to enter Japan?
Most international guests need a visa. Program Only includes guidance resources; Program + Concierge adds hands‑on visa support. Government and embassy fees are paid by you where applicable.
How do I book?
Choose one of the two packages and complete checkout via its Stripe link in the pricing section. Seats are confirmed in order of successful booking (25 max).
What is not included in the price?
International flights to/from Japan, visa processing and embassy fees, personal expenses, and travel insurance (mandatory). Hotel and accommodation are also not included in either package — you arrange your own stay.
Is this retreat suitable for couples?
Yes. However, the retreat is business‑focused. Couples may attend if both are aligned with the retreat purpose.
What airport should I fly into?
Final travel guidance will be provided after booking, but you’ll typically arrive in Japan. VIP airport pickup and group transfers in Japan are included.
Can I extend my stay in Japan?
Absolutely. Many participants choose to add extra days. Our concierge can recommend add‑ons — especially on the Program + Concierge package.
How do I get in touch before booking?
Email products@stephenakintayo.com or call 09134922474 / 07070040482 with questions about packages, visas, or travel.
