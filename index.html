Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on visas, travel, and how booking works.

Do I need a visa to enter Japan?
Most international guests need a visa. Shared bed includes guidance resources; the Single bed package adds hands-on visa support. Government and embassy fees are paid by you where applicable.
How do I book?
Pick shared bed ($12,500) or single bed ($15,000) and complete checkout via the secure Stripe links in the pricing section — each option has its own payment link.
Is this retreat suitable for couples?
Yes. However, the retreat is business‑focused. Couples may attend if both are aligned with the retreat purpose.
What airport should I fly into?
Final travel guidance will be provided, but you’ll typically arrive in Tokyo (HND/NRT) and depart from Osaka (KIX).
Can I extend my stay in Japan?
Absolutely. Many participants choose to add extra days. Our concierge can recommend add‑ons.
